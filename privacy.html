Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Tube Autopilot is a private tool used only by its owner. It uses Google sign-in (OAuth) with the owner's own Google account to:

  • upload videos and thumbnails to the owner's own YouTube channel (YouTube Data API), and
  • read and write the owner's own content-calendar Google Sheet (Google Sheets API).

The tool does not collect, store, sell or share personal data of any other user. Access tokens are stored privately as encrypted secrets and are used only to perform the actions above. The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.
